This Privacy Policy explains how SynCraft ("we," "us," or "our") handles user information in the iOS application SwingCat (the "App").

1. Information we collect and store

The App does not generally require users to directly enter a name, address, phone number, or email address in order to register.

Depending on use of the App, the following information may be stored on your device: timer settings; focus time and timer history; tasks entered by you; cats, items, titles, and other in-app content and their usage; App settings; optional profile information such as a name; and the status of paid features.

We may also collect or process information through third-party services for usage analysis, quality improvements, diagnostics, and advertising. This may include device type, OS, language settings, App version, launch and feature-use events, crash and performance data, IP address and other network information, advertising interactions, and device or advertising identifiers. The information actually processed can vary with your iOS version, device settings, privacy choices, consent status, and third-party service specifications.

2. How we use information

  1. To provide the App and its features.
  2. To understand and analyze App usage.
  3. To improve features, usability, and the user experience.
  4. To investigate and fix errors, crashes, and other issues.
  5. To display advertising and provide advertising-related features.
  6. To provide in-app rewards associated with advertising.
  7. To prevent misuse.
  8. To provide paid features and confirm purchase status.
  9. To respond to inquiries.

3. Firebase

We may use services provided by Google LLC, including Firebase Analytics to understand App usage and improve features, and Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes and improve stability. These services may collect or process usage information, device and OS information, crash logs, App state, and other diagnostic information.

Google's Privacy Policy and other applicable terms apply to information handled by Firebase. Google Privacy Policy
Firebase Privacy and Security

4. Google AdMob

We may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. The App may offer rewarded ads through which users can receive items or other in-app benefits. AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process IP addresses, device information, device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, in-app interactions, and crash or performance information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

Availability of advertising identifiers may depend on device settings, Apple privacy features, and user consent. Google Privacy Policy
How Google uses data from partner sites or apps

5. In-app purchases

We may offer subscriptions and one-time purchase plans through the App Store. Apple processes payments, and we do not directly collect or store credit-card numbers or other payment details. To provide paid features, the App may access or verify information about purchased products and subscription status. Apple Privacy Policy

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to verify purchases, manage subscription and paid-feature access, restore purchases, and understand purchase and subscription activity. RevenueCat processes an App User ID generated by its SDK, purchased product identifiers, transaction and purchase-history information, subscription status and expiration dates, and technical information such as the device operating system and App version.

We use RevenueCat's automatically generated anonymous App User ID rather than setting a name or email address as your purchase identifier. We do not send your in-app profile name or email address to RevenueCat as customer attributes. The use of an anonymous ID does not mean that no information is processed or that purchase records are deleted when you uninstall the App.

For more information, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's information about purchase data.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell information about users. Information may be sent to or processed by Firebase, Google AdMob, Apple, RevenueCat, and other services as necessary to provide the App. We may also disclose information where you consent, where disclosure is required by law, where necessary to protect life, body, or property, or as otherwise permitted by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Timer history, cats, items, titles, settings, and other App data may be stored on your device. Data stored only on a device may be lost if the App is deleted, the device is reset, the device fails, or for other reasons. Data processed by third-party services is managed according to their respective retention periods and policies.

Uninstalling the App does not by itself delete purchase records or other information held by third-party services. For questions or requests about access to or deletion of your information, contact us at hello@syncraft.dev. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law; some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or verify purchases.

8. Advertising and privacy choices

The way advertisements are delivered may vary by region, device settings, Apple privacy features, consent status, and other conditions. Where required, we may display a screen requesting consent or a choice concerning ads or other data uses. You may manage the use of advertising identifiers and other information to the extent available through iOS privacy settings.

9. Children

Minors should use the App with the consent of a parent or legal guardian where necessary. The App is not designed to actively collect personal information such as a child's name, address, phone number, or email address.

10. Security

We endeavor to take reasonable safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, leakage, alteration, loss, and similar events involving information handled through the App.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may change this Policy as needed because of legal changes, changes to App features, changes to third-party services, or other reasons. The revised Policy takes effect when posted in the App or on our website. We will provide notice of material changes where appropriate.
