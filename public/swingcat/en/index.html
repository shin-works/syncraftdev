What is SwingCat?

When you start a timer, a cat leaps for the pendulum.

Stay focused and the cat comes home with you. Stop early and it may fall—or run away.

Time spent focusing becomes a life with cats. SwingCat is a focus timer that makes concentration a little more fun.

Three features

01

Lose focus, and the cat may escape

End a session early and a cat may fall or run away. That little bit of tension can give you one more reason to keep going.

02

Focus to welcome cats home

Finish your timer and a new cat joins your house. The more you focus, the more cats you live with.

03

See your progress add up

Review your focus time, sessions, and streaks to look back on the small steps you take every day.
